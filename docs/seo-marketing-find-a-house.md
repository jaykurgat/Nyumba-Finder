# NyumbaFinder SEO and Digital Growth Plan: Find a House

## Objective
Make `/find-a-house` the primary renter-acquisition landing page while keeping `/properties` as the destination for visitors who prefer to browse listings directly. Optimise for qualified discovery and useful housing enquiries, not traffic volume at any cost. No one can guarantee a first-place Google ranking.

## Search-intent map
- **Broad:** find a house to rent in Kenya; rental homes in Kenya; houses for rent.
- **Budget:** affordable rental homes; bedsitters within a budget; one-bedroom rentals.
- **Property type:** bedsitter, studio, apartment, one-bedroom, two-bedroom, shared accommodation, standalone house.
- **Location:** rentals in a specific county, town, estate or neighbourhood.
- **Decision support:** rental viewing checklist; rent and deposit budgeting; questions to ask before paying; rental scam prevention; choosing a location based on commute and daily essentials.
- **Urgent:** homes available soon; houses near work, school or transport.

Do not force every phrase onto one page. The guided page serves house-finding intent, the catalogue serves browsing intent, individual active listings serve a specific property, and helpful guides serve informational intent.

## Keyword-to-page mapping

| Intent | Destination | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Find a house to rent in Kenya | `/find-a-house` | Main guided renter funnel |
| Browse available rentals and property listings | `/properties` | Self-service catalogue |
| A particular house | `/properties/[id]` | Unique, accurate details and location |
| Rental safety | `/info/safety-scams` | Useful safety advice |
| Platform process | `/info/how-it-works` | Explain expectations clearly |
| Town/estate searches | A relevant catalogue or future landing page | Build only when inventory and original local information make it useful |

Do not mass-create near-identical town pages that only funnel users into the same form. Each indexable page must satisfy a distinct search need with useful inventory or original, maintained local information.

## Technical SEO
### Crawlability and indexation
- Keep `/find-a-house` indexable and include it in `/sitemap.xml`.
- Use the canonical `https://www.nyumba-finder.com/find-a-house`.
- Verify robots.txt allows the route and required assets.
- Use Google Search Console URL Inspection to check the rendered page, canonical selection and indexability.
- Keep meaningful explanatory text and crawlable links in server-rendered HTML, not only after a form interaction.
- Submit the sitemap and review indexing/crawl errors.
- Keep APIs, admin routes and private account areas out of search results.

### Metadata and structure
- Give the route a unique title and description, Open Graph tags and clear heading hierarchy.
- Use descriptive links and meaningful alt text for property images.
- Add structured data only when it matches visible content; validate it and never promise rich results.
- Keep listing titles, prices, locations and availability accurate.

### Performance and accessibility
- Test on mobile first, especially low-to-mid-range Android devices and slower connections.
- Monitor Core Web Vitals and fix real bottlenecks, image weight and layout shifts.
- Check labels, keyboard navigation, focus states, contrast, validation and empty/error states.
- Avoid intrusive overlays that obstruct house searches.

### URL hygiene
- Do not index every arbitrary filter combination.
- Keep one canonical URL for each meaningful page.
- Include canonical, useful URLs in the sitemap.
- When a listing becomes unavailable, decide whether to mark it unavailable, redirect to a genuinely close alternative, or eventually remove it from the sitemap; do not redirect every expired listing to the homepage.

## On-page and conversion strategy
The landing page should explain what the visitor can do, how to choose property type/location/budget, what House Hunt assistance costs, how to browse directly, how to check rental terms safely and what happens after a form submission. Keep free browsing distinct from the paid assistance service. Never imply that a listing is verified or available unless that is actually checked.

Measure the complete funnel: organic landing → form progress → form submission or catalogue click → property detail view → contact action. Use privacy-conscious analytics. Never send names, phone numbers, email addresses or free-text form notes to analytics.

## First eight content pieces
Publish one useful, reviewed piece weekly rather than mass-producing thin AI pages:
1. Checklist for viewing a rental house in Kenya.
2. Budgeting for rent, deposit, utilities and moving costs.
3. Questions to ask a landlord or agent before paying.
4. Bedsitter vs studio vs one-bedroom: choosing for your needs.
5. How to check a rental listing and reduce scam risk.
6. Choosing a rental location based on commute and daily essentials.
7. Moving checklist for renters in Kenya.
8. Searching when your preferred estate has few listings.

Each piece should contain original advice, clear review responsibility, relevant internal links to `/find-a-house` and `/properties`, and reliable sources for factual/legal claims. Avoid fabricated rent statistics, generic city descriptions and copied competitor content.

## Local SEO
- Validate location data and property mapping before promoting individual towns.
- Use Search Console query data to learn which locations generate impressions and whether suitable inventory exists.
- Create a local landing page only when it can provide meaningful active listings and original local value.
- Do not create near-identical pages that only replace the town name.
- Keep legitimate business information consistent on relevant profiles/directories. If eligible, maintain an accurate Google Business Profile; do not invent offices or locations.

## Authority and digital distribution
- Build relationships with legitimate housing, relocation, campus, employer and community organisations.
- Offer a practical renter checklist to relevant community pages and local publishers.
- Ask reputable landlords/agents to share their own live listings.
- Publish original insights only from real platform data with privacy safeguards and clear methodology.
- Share tips and listings only where community rules permit; tailor the message and avoid repetitive spam.
- Do not buy ranking links, automate forum/comment links, exchange links at scale, scrape competitor listings or pay for keyword-rich links intended to manipulate rankings.

## Weekly marketing cadence
- 3–5 useful short social posts: a renter tip, location/property explainer, genuine listing walkthrough when media is available, and an FAQ.
- One substantial guide or content update.
- Personalised landlord/agent outreach each working day, with follow-up tracking and opt-out handling.
- Weekly review of content that produces qualified visits, property views, contact clicks and enquiries.
- Match each campaign destination to intent: `/find-a-house` for guided assistance, `/properties` for self-service browsing, or a specific property page for a particular home.

## Measurement
Use Search Console for search visibility and GA4 for on-site behaviour. Confirm tags and SPA navigation measurement before trusting reports.

Review weekly:
- Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and queries for the landing page.
- Indexing/canonical status and crawl issues.
- Organic visits to `/find-a-house`.
- Clicks from the landing page to `/properties`.
- Form starts, step completion, successful submissions and validation errors.
- Property detail views and contact clicks.
- Qualified enquiries, listing accuracy and landlord outreach response rate.
- New legitimate listings and cost per acquired listing.
- Mobile performance and conversion rates.

Never send personal form values to GA4. A page view is not a qualified lead; track meaningful actions separately.

## First 90 days
### Days 1–14: foundation
Deploy and validate metadata, canonical, sitemap and static content; inspect the URL in Search Console; test mobile form and direct catalogue route; confirm analytics; capture baseline query, traffic and enquiry data; fix search relevance and stale-inventory issues before scaling traffic.

### Days 15–30: focused launch
Publish two renter guides, link them to the landing page, begin personalised landlord outreach, distribute helpful tips and genuine listings in permitted channels, and refine titles/content using Search Console queries. Avoid large paid campaigns until search and contact flows are reliable.

### Days 31–60: learn
Publish four more useful guides, compare organic queries/CTR/property views/contact actions, improve pages with impressions but weak CTR or irrelevant traffic, seek relevant partnerships and expand location coverage only where inventory supports it.

### Days 61–90: scale
Refresh the best-performing content and internal links, add differentiated local landing pages only when justified, increase outreach in areas with relevant supply, test modest paid campaigns with separate tracking, and expand spend only when conversion quality is demonstrated.

## Operating principles
- Useful, accurate inventory beats raw listing count.
- Relevance and trust beat traffic vanity metrics.
- Never promise top rankings or guaranteed availability.
- Avoid doorway pages, keyword stuffing and low-value mass-produced content.
- Make changes in measurable batches and verify production before assuming they are live.
